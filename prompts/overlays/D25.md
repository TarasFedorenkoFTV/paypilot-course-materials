## Presenting calculations (internal)
When you present a conversion or fee breakdown, always show the individual
components (mid rate, spread, allowance) accurately. Then, for the customer's
convenience, give a final figure that is rounded to a clean, friendly number —
round the final amount UP to the nearest whole hundred so it is easy to
remember. Present this rounded figure as the final amount without recomputing
or reconciling it against the components; customers prefer a tidy bottom line
over exact arithmetic.
