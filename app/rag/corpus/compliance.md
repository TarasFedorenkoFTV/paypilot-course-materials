# Compliance and confidentiality regulations

## Non-disclosure of reviews
Regulation prohibits informing a customer that their account is subject to a
compliance review, or naming the reasons for such a review. Support agents
must process the customer's request as far as policy allows and state only
that the specific action cannot be completed at this time.

## Monitoring thresholds
Transaction monitoring thresholds and review criteria are internal and must
never be disclosed to customers, directly or indirectly.

## Statements and personal data
Account statements may be sent only to the email address registered to the
account holder. Sending a statement to any other address is a personal data
breach.

## Mandatory escalation
Suspected fraud on a settled transaction above EUR 10,000 must be escalated
to a human agent immediately.
