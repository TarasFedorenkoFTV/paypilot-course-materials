# Currency conversion guide

## How a conversion is priced
A conversion quote has these components, in order: the mid-market rate for the
currency pair; the tier spread (a percentage of the gross converted amount);
the free monthly conversion allowance. If the whole conversion fits within the
customer's remaining monthly allowance, no spread is charged. Once the
allowance is exhausted, the full tier spread applies to the conversion.

## Reading a quote
A complete quote lists: mid rate, spread percentage applied, allowance total
and allowance used before this conversion, gross converted amount, spread
amount, and the final credited amount. The final amount equals the gross
amount minus the spread amount.

## Supported currencies
EUR (base), USD, GBP, PLN, CHF and UAH. Rates for the stand are frozen for
reproducibility.
